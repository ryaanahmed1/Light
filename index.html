iPhone setup

On your Mac, put this file in its own folder as index.html. Drag the folder onto Netlify Drop and follow the prompts to get a web address. Anyone with that address can view the page. Your checklist and count stay in your browser.

Open that HTTPS address in Bluefy on your iPhone. Allow Bluetooth, plug in the strip, and close its usual control app. Tap Connect light and choose the device starting with KS03~. Other controller models need their own commands.

Use the same address and browser each morning to keep your progress. The counter records successful power or color commands and skips repeated settings during a connection.
